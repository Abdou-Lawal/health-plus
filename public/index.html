This is not a diagnosis. Health+ helps you prepare for a visit to a health professional. In an emergency, call your local emergency services.

Know what to tell your doctor.

Health+ helps you prepare a medical visit. Describe your symptoms, add a photo of a prescription or a skin area, and get a clear summary to show to a health professional. It keeps working with a weak or no connection.

How it works

  1. Describe

    Write how you feel, in English or French. Warning signs such as chest pain or trouble breathing open an emergency screen at once, using fixed rules and not AI.

  2. On-device AI

    Gemini Nano, built into Chrome, turns your words into a structured summary on your own device. Nothing is sent.

  3. Cloud, only if you agree

    If the on-device model is not available, or for photos, you see exactly what would be sent and you choose. A badge shows which AI answered.

  4. Show your summary

    Take the summary to a health professional. It is saved only on your device.

Private by design

  • Your symptom text stays on your device by default.
  • Photos lose their location data before anything is sent.
  • A skin photo needs a detailed description first.
  • Health+ never diagnoses, never names a disease and never suggests a treatment.

Why it matters

“Health is a state of complete physical, mental and social well-being and not merely the absence of disease or infirmity.”

Constitution of the World Health Organization, 1946

“As to diseases, make a habit of two things — to help, or at least to do no harm.”

Hippocrates, Epidemics, Book I (translation by W. H. S. Jones)

In 1978 the Declaration of Alma-Ata made primary health care the heart of “Health for All”. Health+ is a preparation tool, not a medical device, and it does not replace a health professional.
